4. Cookies and tracking

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We also use similar tools, including tracking pixels, tags and local storage. We call all of these "cookies" in this section. We group them by what they do:

  • Strictly necessary: required for the site to work. Always on.
  • Functional: enable features like chat, booking and video.
  • Analytics: help us understand how the site is used.
  • Marketing: let us and our ad partners measure ads and show relevant ones.

Non-essential cookies stay off until you choose to turn them on.

5. Email and text messages

Email

We send marketing emails only to people who asked for them, or where the law allows us to because of an existing business relationship.

Under CASL, an existing business relationship covers two years after your last purchase or contract with us, or six months after you inquire. Before that period ends, we'll ask whether you'd like to keep hearing from us.

Every marketing email identifies us, includes our mailing address, and has an unsubscribe link. We process unsubscribe requests promptly, and within 10 business days at most. After you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a suppression list only so we don't email you again.

Our emails may contain a small tracking pixel and tracked links. These tell us whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, which helps us send more useful content. Most email apps let you block images to prevent this.

You'll still receive transactional or service messages, such as replies to your inquiry, invoices and project updates, even after unsubscribing from marketing.

Text messages

If you opt in to text messages, we'll send messages about {{SMS_TOPICS}}. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not share mobile phone numbers or text-message opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers who help us deliver messages.
